# Code of Conduct

Global Service Studio adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel the project uses.

## In short

- Be respectful and patient. Many contributors are volunteers serving their own communities.
- Assume good intent, and give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualized language or publishing of private information.
- People of every background, denomination, belief and level of experience are welcome to use and contribute to this software.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project owner, [@Isaacpaezz](https://github.com/Isaacpaezz), or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, issue or pull request. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone who does so, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
